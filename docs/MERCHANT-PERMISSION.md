## Future real-merchant publication

If the user later wants a real merchant in a public build or submission, stop
and open a new permission/terms gate before changing runtime, copy, or media.
At minimum, the private evidence should record:

- the merchant's legal or trading name;
- the name and role of the person granting permission;
- a dated, affirmative written response;
- the exact catalogue endpoints and public surfaces covered;
- whether name, logo, product data, images, screenshots, quotes, video, and
  handoff links are permitted;
- approved descriptive wording and attribution;
- any expiry, territory, removal, or review conditions; and
- a private evidence location and reviewer.

Do not commit private contact details or correspondence. Do not turn a verbal
relationship, public endpoint, or lack of objection into claimed consent.
